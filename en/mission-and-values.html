Mission and values

What the association sets out to do, for whom, and under what rules. This page follows chapters 2, 3, 4, 9 and 14 of the specifications, and adds nothing to them.

The objectives

  • Welcome divorced mothers in a dignified, calm and secure setting.
  • Support personal rebuilding, self-confidence and emotional balance.
  • Offer regular and accessible well-being activities.
  • Strengthen the social bond between women going through similar situations.
  • Support parenting after a separation or a divorce.
  • Refer people to qualified professionals or to the relevant services when that is needed.

Who the association welcomes

  • Who is welcomed

    Divorced or separated mothers, with or without dependent children, women currently going through a separation, isolated mothers, and women who need a safe place to speak about a family breakdown.

  • Which needs

    Listening, well-being, moral support, parenting support and social guidance.

  • In what framework

    Confidentiality, respect, neutrality, absence of judgment. A non-political, non-adversarial framework.

The missions

  • Reception and listening

    A first contact whose purpose is to identify what each person needs and to suggest a suitable pathway — not to fill in a file.

  • Well-being and relief

    Activities for relaxation, breathing, self-esteem, gentle movement and emotional balance.

  • Parenting support

    Rebuilding family organisation, communication with children and day-to-day stability.

  • Social guidance

    Informing people and connecting them with the appropriate services, without standing in for them.

  • Social connection

    Mutual support, speech and solidarity, through meetings, discussion groups and group activities.

The operating rules

  • Strict confidentiality over every personal situation.
  • No judgment about anyone's family, social or personal background.
  • No publication of a photograph, a name or a testimonial without explicit permission.
  • Referral to a qualified professional in sensitive or urgent situations.
  • Respect for schedules, facilitators, other participants and any children present.
  • A non-political, non-adversarial framework, centred on human support and well-being.

What the association will measure

Chapter 14 sets out what the association will track in order to know whether it is useful. These are indicators to be measured, not results already achieved: no figure is published on this site until one has actually been recorded.

  • Number of mothers welcomed.
  • Number of workshops and discussion groups held.
  • Rate of participation in activities.
  • Anonymous feedback from the people welcomed.
  • Number of active partnerships.
  • Quality of human follow-up and regularity of the calendar.
  • Change in social connection and in the support participants feel.

The association is not a medical service, a legal service or an emergency service. If you or a child are in immediate danger, do not use this site: call the emergency services. The number to be shown here depends on the country the association will operate in, which is still to be set.

Nothing entrusted to the association is published — no photograph, no name, no testimonial — without written permission.
