Accessibility

Some of the people who read this site will do so on an old phone, in a badly lit room, in a hurry. Accessibility is not a checkbox here; it is the condition for the page to be of any use.

What has been measured

  • Measured contrasts

    Every text colour was photographed against the background actually painted behind it, in a browser, rather than inferred from the stylesheet. The lowest on the site is recorded in the verification report.

  • Keyboard navigation

    A skip link comes first, a visible focus outline everywhere, and the menu closes with the Escape key.

  • No horizontal overflow

    Checked at nineteen screen widths, from 320 to 1440 pixels, in both languages.

  • Heading hierarchy

    One first-level heading per page, and no skipped levels.

  • Reduced motion

    Animations stop when the system asks for reduced motion.

  • Readable without scripts

    The content, the links and the form text stay readable when JavaScript is switched off.

What has not been

  • No test with a real screen reader

    The markup is sound, but nobody has listened to these pages. That is not the same thing.

  • No conformance audit

    No WCAG level is claimed. Claiming a conformance that has not been audited is an unverifiable statement.

  • No review by the people concerned

    The wording of this site has not been read back by women who have been through a separation. That is the review that is missing most.

Any barrier found on this site will be reportable as soon as a contact line exists; it will be treated as a defect, not as a preference.
