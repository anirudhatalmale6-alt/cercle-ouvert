Workshop calendar

This page is deliberately empty of dates.

A calendar is the most-used page on an association's site, and it is also the only one that cannot be filled in ahead of time without lying. An invented date makes someone leave home for nothing; a mother who arranged childcare in order to come does not come back a second time.

What the specifications do give are indicative frequencies, and they are reproduced as they stand on the Services and activities page. What they do not give — the day, the time, the place, the date of the first session — is still to be set and is flagged as such below.

The stated rhythms

Activity Purpose Indicative frequency
Gentle yoga and stretchingPhysical relaxation and less stressWeekly
Guided meditationMental calm and emotional groundingWeekly
Discussion groupExpression, listening and mutual supportWeekly or twice monthly
Group walkSocial connection and gentle physical activityMonthly
Self-esteem workshopConfidence and personal rebuildingMonthly
Mother-and-child activityStrengthening the family bond after separationMonthly
Nutrition and healthy living workshopEveryday balance and preventionQuarterly

“Indicative” is the word used in the specifications, and it is kept as it stands: these rhythms describe an intended rhythm of organisation, not a commitment to hold a given session.

What is still to be set

  • To be setThe venueAddress, access, floor, lift, and whether a child can come along. Chapter 10.
  • To be setThe day and time of each activitySeven activities, seven slots. The specifications give a rhythm, not a timetable.
  • To be setThe date of the first sessionIt depends on the venue and on the facilitators.
  • To be setHow to sign upIn person, by phone, through the form — and whether signing up is needed at all.
  • To be setClosing periodsSchool holidays and public holidays in the country of operation, which is still to be set.

As soon as those five points are settled, this page will show a real calendar: one line per session, with the place, the time and how to join. It is already built to receive them.
