Volunteering and partnerships

The internal structure stays simple, clear and service-oriented. Roles adapt to the size of the association and to the number of volunteers available.

The internal roles

  • General coordination

    Overseeing the service and the activity calendar.

  • Reception and listening

    First contact, guidance and discreet follow-up.

  • Well-being

    Planning workshops and coordinating facilitators.

  • Parenting support

    Mother-and-child activities and themed meetings.

  • Partnerships

    Relations with social, legal, educational and health-related organisations.

  • Volunteering

    Reception, logistics, support and facilitation, depending on each person's training.

The partnerships sought

  • Social centres, local councils, neighbourhood centres and family support organisations.
  • Psychologists, family mediators, specialised educators and social workers.
  • Well-being professionals: coaches, yoga teachers, relaxation practitioners and nutritionists.
  • Schools, libraries, cultural centres and local associations.
  • Administrative guidance services and organisations supporting women.

What is still to be set
