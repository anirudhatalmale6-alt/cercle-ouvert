Services and activities

Eight services, seven regular activities and a five-step pathway. The rhythms below are the ones set out in the specifications; the actual dates will live on the Calendar page, and they are still to be set.

What is offered

  • Individual reception, by appointment or during an open session.
  • Discussion groups facilitated by a designated responsible person.
  • Workshops on managing stress and emotions.
  • Relaxation, meditation, breathing and gentle yoga activities.
  • Self-esteem, self-image and confidence workshops.
  • Mother-and-child activities, to strengthen the family bond.
  • Referral to qualified professionals according to each situation.
  • Themed meetings on parenting, personal rebuilding and life after a divorce.

The support pathway

  1. First contact

    By phone, by message, through the form on this site, or during an open session. There is nothing to prepare.

  2. Orientation conversation

    A confidential conversation to identify the main need. It is not an assessment, and nothing is written down without your knowledge.

  3. Suggested pathway

    Signing up for whichever activities are useful, and referral to an outside service if the situation calls for it.

  4. Participation

    In groups, workshops and meetings, as your availability allows. Coming once and then stopping is an acceptable answer.

  5. Human follow-up

    A regular check-in with a responsible person when the situation calls for it — never imposed.

What is still to be set

  • To be setWhere the activities take placeChapter 10: a confidential reception room and a multipurpose room are required. No address will be written here before a venue is confirmed.
  • To be setThe facilitatorsYoga, meditation and nutrition facilitators are professionals still to be recruited (chapter 13). No name, qualification or diploma will be shown without documentary proof.
  • To be setSigning upHow someone signs up for a workshop, and whether signing up is required at all, depends on the venue and on group size.
  • To be setChildcareThe specifications provide for a safe children's corner during mother-and-child activities. Whether one exists during the other activities changes everything for an isolated mother.
