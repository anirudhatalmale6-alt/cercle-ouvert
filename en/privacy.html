Privacy and data protection

This page states what is true of this site today, and what remains to be decided before it goes live. It is not a copied privacy-policy template: half the sentences in a template would be false here.

This page is not legal advice. It must be reviewed by someone qualified in the country of operation before the site goes live, particularly on personal-data protection and on information relating to children.

What is true today

  • No cookies, no analytics

    The site sets no cookie and carries no analytics tool. That is not an intention but a measurement: the test suite counts the outbound requests of every page, and there are none.

  • No external resources

    The typefaces are served from the site itself. A typeface loaded from a third party sends that third party the address of the page being read — on this site, that would reveal a great deal more than a reading preference.

  • The form sends nothing

    It checks the fields inside the browser and stops there. No data is transmitted, stored or kept.

  • The quick-exit button

    It replaces the current page with a search engine, without leaving it in the session history. It does not erase pages already visited: they stay in the browser history until someone clears it.

  • No photograph, no name, no testimonial

    There is none on this site, and there will be none without written permission from the person concerned — chapter 9.

What must be decided before launch

  • To be setThe data controllerWho is legally answerable for the processing: the legal entity, its name and its address.
  • To be setThe country and the applicable lawThe data-protection regime, the mandatory notices and people's rights differ from one country to another.
  • To be setWho reads the reception requestsOne person, a team, a shared mailbox? The answer determines what can be promised.
  • To be setHow long information is keptHow long a request is kept, and what happens to it afterwards.
  • To be setHostingWhere the site and the messages will be hosted, and in which country.
  • To be setThe permissions registerHow permission to publish a photograph or a testimonial is obtained, kept and withdrawn.
